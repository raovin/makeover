# Provider mark sources

- `Claude-Mark-32.png` is the current transparent Claude favicon published by
  Claude at <https://cdn.sanity.io/images/4zrzovbb/claude-com/369b14e80ac643cc09dccd581ccb91f82b559190-32x32.png>.
- `Grok-Mark-144.png` is a transparent, light-on-dark rasterization of the two
  foreground paths in Grok's current official favicon at
  <https://grok.com/images/favicon.svg>. The vector's exact 400 by 400 foreground
  bounds were centered into a 144 by 144 bitmap with a two-pixel safety margin.

The menu bar uses these small official marks directly instead of approximating
their silhouettes with generic line primitives.
